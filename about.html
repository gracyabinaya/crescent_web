Who We Are

21 Years of Technology Innovation & Human Empowerment

Crescent Technosoft was founded in 2005 by Syed Mohammed Mahe Alam, a trained software engineer who set out to build more than a software company — an institution that would globalize technology and uplift lives.

Guided by strong family values and a deep respect for knowledge instilled by his father, the educationalist Syed Badre Alam, he built the company on a dual mission: implementing high-tier technology solutions worldwide while transforming raw graduates into high-performing software professionals.
