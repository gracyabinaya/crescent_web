300+ Active Hospital Installations

CRESCENT HMS (Hospital Management System)

An end-to-end enterprise hospital software operating across 300+ healthcare institutions in India, UAE, Saudi Arabia, and Africa. Automates clinical, administrative, and financial workflows from admission to discharge.

  • OPD / IPD & Emergency Management: Real-time bed availability monitoring, ward shifting, and clinical charting.
  • Oncology & Radiotherapy Sheets: Specialized treatment planning charts for oncologists, physicists, and technologists.
  • TPA Cashless Claim & Billing: Tariff master management, IP advance collection tracking, and GST cash audits.
Clinical Lab & Diagnostic Software

CRESCENT LMS (Clinical Lab Software)

Designed specifically for Clinical Laboratories and Diagnostic Pathology Centers. Generate barcoded sample tags, interface directly with lab analyzer equipment, and dispatch instant diagnostic PDF reports via WhatsApp and SMS.

  • Barcoded Specimen Tracking: Eliminates mislabeling errors in blood, urine, and pathology samples.
  • WhatsApp & SMS Report Delivery: Direct electronic PDF report dispatch to patient phones.
  • Automated Analyzer Interfacing: Auto-captures test readings directly from diagnostic machinery.
Polyclinic & Multi-Chain Specialist

CRESCENT CMS (Clinic & Doctor Software)

Tailored for single doctor clinics and multi-specialty polyclinics across India. Streamline patient appointment booking, token queue management, consultation billing, and rapid digital prescription writing.

  • Fast Digital E-Prescription Writer: Instant medicine master lookup and saved prescription templates.
  • Patient Queue & Token Display: Live TV monitor integration for patient waiting room queue control.
  • Multi-Location Standardization: Real-time doctor scheduling and standardized branch pricing.
Retail & Wholesale Pharmacy Suite

CRESCENT Pharma (Pharmacy Management)

Comprehensive retail and wholesale pharmacy software with batch-wise stock control, near-expiry alerts, purchase indent forms, barcode POS billing, and GST-compliant financial accounting statements.

  • Batch & Expiry Alert System: Proactive alerts before medicines reach expiration date.
  • Barcode POS Billing: Rapid item scanning with automatic discount calculations and thermal receipts.
  • Re-Order Stock Triggers: Automated purchase order alerts when stock hits minimum reorder levels.
Plastic Processing & Manufacturing ERP

EdTech ERP & Campus Suite
